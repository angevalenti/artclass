Quick Reference

Visual Art I is a one-semester introductory course that moves through drawing, painting, printmaking, and sculpture before culminating in a collaborative hallway mural. You will build real skills, make real art, and leave real work installed in this school. Grading is based on process, effort, craftsmanship, and growth — not talent.

Safety is non-negotiable. You will be trained on every tool before you touch it. X-Acto knives, brayers, paint supplies, and cleanup procedures all have specific protocols. Violations result in loss of tool privileges. This is a working studio, not a hangout — respect the space and the materials.

All materials are provided by the art department. You do not need to purchase anything for this class. Bring yourself and your effort — everything else is here.

Line, shape, form, value, color, texture, space. Contrast, emphasis, balance, rhythm, unity, movement, proportion. These are the words you'll use every day. They're not tests — they're tools for talking about what you see and what you make.
