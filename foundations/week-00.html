Build Your Sketchbook.

Before we draw, paint, or print anything — we build the book that holds it all. Hand-cut, hand-folded, hand-stitched. This sketchbook is yours from the first page to the last stitch.

01 photo: cutting, folding & pressing signatures
Phase 1 — already in progress

Cut, Fold, Punch, Press

✂️

Cut your sheets to size

Each sheet gets cut to the same dimensions. Measure twice — a crooked cut shows in the final book.

📐

Fold into signatures

Stack 4–5 sheets together, fold as a group. Use the bone folder to crease — fingernails leave a sloppy fold.

📍

Punch your holes

Use the template to mark, then punch with the awl. Same spacing on every signature — this is what makes the stitch line up.

📚

Press the signatures flat

Stack them under weight overnight. Pressed signatures sit tight and stitch clean.

02 photo: decorating & personalizing covers
Phase 2 — this week

Create Your Covers

📏

Cut your cover boards

Front and back covers, same size as your signatures. Slightly larger is fine — a tiny overhang protects the pages.

🎨

Design your cover

Collage, paint, draw, stamp — this is the face of your sketchbook for the whole semester. Make it yours.

📍

Punch matching holes

Same template as the signatures. Cover holes must line up exactly or the stitch won't work.

03 photo: coptic stitch binding in progress
Phase 3 — bringing it all together

Coptic Stitch Binding

🪡

Thread your needle

Cut a long piece of waxed thread — about 4 arm-lengths. Thread and pull halfway. No knot yet.

📖

Attach the back cover

Start from the outside of the back cover. Go through the first hole, into the first signature, and back out. Wrap around the stitch on the cover to lock it.

🔗

Chain stitch the signatures

Each new signature links to the one below it. In through the hole, out, loop under the previous stitch, pull tight. Repeat across, then add the next signature.

✅

Attach the front cover & tie off

Last signature connects to the front cover the same way you started. Tie a square knot inside the last signature. Trim the tail.
