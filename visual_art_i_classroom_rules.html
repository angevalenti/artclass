🎨 Visual Art I Studio Rules

Let's build a creative, respectful space together

📱 No Phone Use

Your phone stays in your backpack or locker during class. This keeps you focused on your creative work and respects the learning environment for everyone.

🤝 Respect Everyone

Treat this classroom and your classmates with kindness. Listen when others are speaking. Don't mock work. Be someone people want to create with.

⚠️ Professional Boundaries

I'm your teacher, not your friend or therapist. I care about you and want you to succeed, but I have professional limits. If you're struggling, I'll help connect you with a counselor or trusted adult who can really help. My job is to teach you art and help you find your creative voice.
